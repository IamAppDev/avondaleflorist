About the shop

Started with one bucket of tulips and a folding table

Avondale Florist opened in 2011 when Amara Okafor started selling bouquets from a folding table outside the old hardware store on 6th. The shop moved indoors two years later, but the high ceilings and big storefront windows stayed the same.

We still work with three growers within a few hours of the city, which means the shop changes with the seasons — dahlias crowd the counter in September, and by January it's mostly branches and bulbs.

Today it's Amara, her son Femi, and two arrangers, all working out of the same corner shop.

— The Okafor family
